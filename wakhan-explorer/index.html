Reading the tracks. Solid intervals show inferred CN over read-depth dots. Copies use the left axis; depth uses the calibrated right axis. Paired HP tracks share a CN range that adapts after navigation pauses. Raw bins outside this range are clipped. Hatched centromeric masks contain neither HP depth points nor inferred CN rules.

Folded BAF spans 0–0.5. Grey zero bins have unknown SNP support. Plain gray regions lack source CN segments; a coloured interval at zero is a reported zero CN state. The compact LOH track shows Wakhan calls; hatching identifies masked portions that are withheld from the LOH encoding.

Wakhan analysed autosomes only. Unphased depth is not available as a trustworthy genome-wide series in this export. CN confidence comes from the BED files and is not phasing confidence.

Gene labels show NCG 7.2 canonical cancer drivers and are prioritized by their number of distinct supporting publications. This ranks label placement rather than statistical significance or relevance to HCC1954.

This proof of concept uses published analysis artifacts. Coverage and BAF were recovered from literal JSON in the archived Plotly figure because native tables were not included. The visualization could later be adapted to standard Wakhan result bundles.
